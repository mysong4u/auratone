Thank you for your review. We are happy to provide detailed answers to your questions to clarify our simple and standard business model. 

AuraTone is a standard **Paid App (Upfront purchase to download)**. There are no In-App Purchases, no subscriptions, no accounts, and no external web-payment systems. Once a user purchases and downloads the app from the App Store, 100% of the features are permanently unlocked and run entirely locally.

All users who purchase and download AuraTone from the App Store. Users only purchase the app itself on the iOS App Store prior to downloading. There are no other payment gateways, websites, or external stores. We do not offer any In-App Purchases, subscriptions, or upgrades within the app.

3. What specific types of previously purchased features can a user access in the app?
None. Users do not access any previously purchased features, digital goods, or content from any other platform or website. The app is completely standalone, offline-capable, and does not require any user accounts or cloud login sessions.

4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?
None. There is absolutely no paid content, subscription model, or hidden premium feature unlocked within the app that bypasses the App Store's payment system. AuraTone is a standard "Pay-to-Download" product, where 100% of its music-editing and ringtone-export features are fully unlocked locally upon purchase on the App Store.
